What you're agreeing to

HappyBrowsing is a commitment device. When you arm it, it removes administrator rights from your account on purpose, blocks parts of some websites and closes some apps. By installing and arming it, you accept that this is what it's for.

Use it on your own account

  • Install it only on a computer you own or are allowed to manage.
  • Arm it only for your own account, or for someone else's account with their knowledge.

Getting out

  • Request an unlock in the HappyBrowsing app and wait 30 minutes. You're an administrator for the next 2 hours.
  • While unlocked, you can remove everything: sudo bash /Library/HappyBrowsing/uninstall.sh on a Mac, or run uninstall.ps1 from C:\Program Files\HappyBrowsing in an administrator Terminal on Windows.
  • HappyBrowsing is the only thing that gives admin back. If it ever stopped working while armed, getting admin back could mean erasing and reinstalling macOS or resetting Windows. Keep a backup.

Other websites and services

HappyBrowsing changes what your own browser shows you. YouTube, Reddit, Instagram, NextDNS and the extensions it installs have their own terms, and following them is up to you. The extensions are made by other developers and come from the Chrome Web Store.
